# NULL Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026.

## What the app collects

NULL is an offline, single-player iPhone game. It does not collect or transmit personal data. It does not require an account, include advertising or analytics, or track players.

## Settings stored on your iPhone

NULL stores your language, sound effects, combat effects, and portrait motion preferences locally so it can remember them the next time you play. NULL does not send these settings to the developer. To remove the app's local settings, delete the app from your iPhone.

## Support email and this website

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and any information you choose to include. That information is used to respond and troubleshoot. It is retained as needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask the developer to delete your correspondence by emailing the address below.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.
